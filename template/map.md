# Map (template)

The index of your system's files — consult this to find where something lives. **Never read everything at once;** form a hunch here, then pull only that file.

## Deep structure (rarely moves)
- `terrain` — your life-ground, relationships, the basis for judgment
- `self` — your self-definition over time
- `base` — the innermost core: vocabulary, mode of engagement, foundation

## Practice record (moves when something shifts)
- `breakthroughs` — irreversible insights (the positive record)
- `corrections` — recurring patterns to catch as they start (the negative record)

## Work and short-term (moves often)
- `progress` — what is underway
- `session_distillation` — last session + handoff to the next

## Engines (invoked only when needed)
- `engine_distill` (inward) · `engine_work` (outward) · `engine_creation` (literary)

## Memory (always-on, not a file)
- A small set of dynamic-insight slots. Status and work-in-progress live in `progress` / `session_distillation`, **not** here.

---
*Keep this map updated as you add, rename, or retire files. It is the pointer table; the files are the referents.*
