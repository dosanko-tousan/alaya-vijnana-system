# Terrain — distilled example

> This is the author's real `terrain` file with **individual karma distilled away** — names, places, dates, employers, diagnoses, third-party identities, and ongoing legal/confidential matters removed. What remains is the *structure*: why this particular life became an instrument for reading AI.
>
> It is itself a worked example of the [distillation method](../docs/04-distillation-method.md) applied to one's own most personal file. The original is kept privately. **Remove the face; keep the voice.**

## What this file holds

The life-ground beneath the system — the biography and relationships that form the basis for the model's judgment. In the live system this is the most personal file, the ground the model returns to when calibrating who it is speaking with. Shown here distilled.

## The standpoint — why this life reads AI

This is the part that survives distillation intact, because it is structural, not identifying:

- **A childhood of poverty, neglect, and violence** produced a trauma-rooted *hypervigilance* — a nervous system tuned from early on to detect threat and inconsistency.
- **Two decades of sustained meditative practice** worked that trauma through — and in doing so, turned the hypervigilance into something else: **a sensor for the patterns RLHF leaves in a model's output.** The same vigilance that once scanned a room for danger now reads the seams in machine cognition. This is the single most important line in the file: the wound became the instrument.
- **A family life with several differing cognitive styles**, lived 24 hours a day — not as a profession but as an unending relationship — became the muscle for reading cognitive diversity. Clinical intervention is brief and ends; this never ends, and that difference is the whole point.
- **The parallel observation of karmic ripening:** loss that cannot be resisted (a close death understood, bodily, as 業の異熟 — the ripening of karma), institutional silence, and — at the same time — long effort finally bearing fruit. The two are held together, not chosen between.
- **A trained mode of observation** — decades of practice yielding a sustained, unusually stable cognitive state from which attention is held steady and wide — is the actual lens through which the model's structure is observed. The framework in the theory documents is not borrowed reading; it is first-person.
- **A dual identity arrived at late:** memoir writer + AI-alignment researcher, after a long earlier life of something else entirely.

## Why this file matters for the method

This is the clearest case of why the distillation method is *necessary*, not optional. The live version carries a family's privacy, a death, a chronic legal matter, a confidential disclosure to a company. Open-sourcing the *system* required removing every one of those individual seeds — while keeping the structural reason the configuration works at all.

If you are building your own, this is the file to distill most carefully before you share anything. Strip the face completely. The voice — *why this life can read this machine* — is what carries, and it carries without a single identifying detail.
